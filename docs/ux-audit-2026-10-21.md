# UX audit 2026-10-21 (Nielsen's ten heuristics)

Started from ten screenshots the owner flagged. Numbers in brackets are Nielsen's heuristics:
H1 visibility of system status · H2 match with the real world · H3 user control and freedom · H4 consistency and
standards · H5 error prevention · H6 recognition rather than recall · H7 flexibility and efficiency · H8 aesthetic and
minimalist design · H9 help users recognise, diagnose and recover from errors · H10 help and documentation.

House rules that also apply (memory): filters are round tag.observe.tw chips, no boxed forms; explanatory small print
goes in the foot 本頁的資料來源與計算方式 block; empty data is left blank; no repeated per-item labels.

## Root cause behind screenshots 1–2

The shared host runs at load 25–60 with ~15 GB swap in use (MariaDB is the top consumer). `/api/topics?view=home`
measured 15.5 s once (later 1.1 s); the web's public reads time out at 10 s. The homepage streamed the topic ranking in
a Suspense whose failure threw to `app/error.tsx`, so one slow sidebar read replaced the whole homepage with
「資料暫時無法載入」. `/relations/?days=90` failed the same way once.

## Not changed (needs the owner or the backend)

- **Topic quality**: 經驗、風格、感動、價格、Pro、HOME rank high on /topics/. These are vocabulary/stop-word decisions, not UI.
- **Host capacity**: section failures now stay local, but they will keep happening while the host swaps. The read
  timeout (10 s) was left as is.
- **Graph search**: a box to find and focus one blog in the graph would help further; not done.
- **Period switches** (近 7／30／90 天) still use SectionTabs: by the house rule, view switches are tabs and narrowing
  filters are chips.
